# V3.2+ Capability Catalog

**Status:** OWNER-DIRECTED PLANNING — comprehensive catalog recorded 2026-08-29.

**Purpose:** provide one classified inventory for every capability carried beyond V3.1.
This catalog is a planning source, not proof that a feature, provider, migration, release,
or product decision exists.

---

## 1. Classification and delivery rules

| Class | Meaning | May engineering start? |
|---|---|---|
| `CORE` | Owner-directed product capability with an agreed place in the programme | After its named discovery decisions and entry criteria |
| `PRODUCT` | Behaviour or commercial policy is unresolved | No; the owner must approve the recorded decisions first |
| `EXTERNAL` | Needs a selected provider, credentials, contract, or production rail | Interfaces/local fallbacks may be built; live claims wait for evidence |
| `EVIDENCE` | Adds operational complexity only justified by measured load or usage | No; a written trigger and measured baseline are required |
| `QUALITY` | Completes usability, accessibility, privacy, or operational maturity | Yes when its prerequisite surface exists |
| `RETIRED` | Intentionally removed, replaced, or prohibited | No; requires a new owner decision and architecture review to return |

An item can carry more than one class. Its strictest gate controls. `Target` means the
earliest intended programme slot, not a release promise.

## 10. Programme completeness and change control

The catalog is complete against the V3 gap register, migration matrix, V3.1 reconciliation,
Phase E closure, and the owner-restored AI/chat direction as of 2026-08-29. A newly proposed
capability must be added here before implementation with:

1. owner and user outcome;
2. class and earliest target phase;
3. product, external, and evidence gates;
4. domain source of truth and reused engines;
5. privacy/security/subject-data treatment;
6. verification level and explicit non-goals.

Completeness means every known capability has a disposition. It does not convert conditional
items into approved scope and does not override external enablement or release gates.
