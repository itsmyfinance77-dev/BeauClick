## Purpose

This packet closes the product values that engineering must not invent. It
applies the owner's three connected directions as one commercial model:

1. customers are not charged a BeauClick booking fee; seller subscriptions are
   the primary recurring revenue;
2. booking collection supports pay at venue, online deposit with venue balance,
   and full online payment;
3. cancellation, late cancellation, no-show, provider fault, reschedule,
   dispute and seller settlement are evaluated from the immutable policy the
   customer accepted.

ADR-039 and the v1 contract already preserve these extension points. This
packet does not authorise a gateway, payout rail, production money movement or
legal claim. It separates decisions so one later change does not require
rewriting Booking, Payment, Commerce and Financial together.

## Current repository facts

- Booking is professional/slot keyed and already emits lifecycle facts.
- The current checkout collects one full order total.
- Payment verifies the exact amount captured by a provider.
- Booking cancellation currently causes the remaining captured amount to be
  refunded; `no_show` has no financial consequence.
- Financial currently applies a 1,500-basis-point commission configuration to
  platform-observed money and has no seller-pending/disputed availability
  schedule.
- No seller subscription, plan entitlement, booking allowance, deposit
  schedule or production payout adapter exists.
- The commercial-policy foundation is intentionally not composed into the API
  and has no production provider.

## Recommendation summary

| Decision | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| revenue | customer booking free; seller subscription primary | predictable B2B revenue without reducing customer conversion |
| packaging | seller chooses an admin-defined plan or buys a custom credit quantity | supports both predictable bundles and pay-for-needed-capacity |
| allowance | plan-included credits and custom purchases share one ledger but retain their source | 200 may be configured or entered, never a system constant |
| overage | warn, then seller explicitly buys more credits; never break existing bookings | supports changing demand without a hidden charge or operational lockout |
| collection | all three v1 modes, selected only within platform-approved bounds | supports cash-heavy and online sellers without lying about collected money |
| deposit | seller chooses fixed or percentage inside platform bounds | commercial flexibility with consistent customer protection |
| customer cancellation | full deposit refund before cutoff; approved retention after cutoff | clear, predictable consequence |
| no-show | retain the approved deposit amount after slot end only | compensates reserved capacity without charging an uncollected balance |
| provider/platform fault | 100% refund of platform-collected service money | the customer did not cause the failure |
| dispute | hold seller availability while the dispute is open | prevents payout/refund races |
| settlement | pending, disputed, available, settled, refunded and reversed are append-only facts | honest accounting and replay safety |
| venue balance | seller-reported only; excluded from platform-collected revenue | BeauClick has no authoritative collection fact |

## Decisions requiring approval

### `V33-DEC-009` — Admin-defined plans and selectable booking credits

**Structure ratified 2026-09-02.** The binding structural wording is in the
decision register. The blanks below remain open under #46.

**Owner clarification, 2026-09-02.** BeauClick has two purchase paths. A seller
may select one of the subscription plans defined by an administrator, or enter
a desired booking-credit quantity and buy that quantity directly. A plan holder
may also buy custom additional credits later. No allowance, including 200, is a
permanent system constant.

**Recommendation.** Use a combined plan-plus-top-up model:

- every seller has a minimal base workspace; paid capability is then acquired
  through a plan, a custom credit purchase, or both;
- an administrator creates an immutable plan version with public name, price,
  billing term, included booking credits, staff seats, locations and feature
  entitlements such as AI, SMS or analytics;
- a seller may select one active plan version; its included credits are granted
  once per subscription term;
- instead of a paid plan, a seller may purchase a positive integer custom
  booking-credit quantity within platform-approved minimum and maximum bounds;
- a plan holder may use the same custom purchase path as a top-up;
- a plan may include 200 credits if the administrator configures that value;
  changing it creates a new plan version and affects only future purchases or
  renewals;
- top-up checkout may offer quick presets such as 50, 100, 200 or 500, plus an
  allowed custom quantity; presets are presentation only;
- a versioned top-up price schedule maps quantity to unit price or volume tier;
- each grant records its source as `plan_included` or `custom_purchase`; each
  purchase snapshots quantity, unit/tier price, total, currency, schedule
  version and term, so later price changes affect only later purchases;
- professional and business subscribers use the same quota mechanism; plan,
  vertical and identity type do not change the meaning of one booking credit.

This keeps pricing flexible without making price a mutable field on a live
subscription or scattering plan names through Booking.

**Values to approve:** minimal base-workspace capabilities `________________`;
initial plan versions and their prices/included credits/
seats/locations/capabilities `________________`; minimum top-up quantity `____`;
maximum self-service quantity `____`; top-up price tiers `________________`;
whether unused included and top-up credits expire, carry forward, or both under
a stated cap `________________`; annual prepaid option and discount `________`.

**Recommendation on billing term.** Use an anniversary-based term measured
from the subscription start instant, not a Gregorian or Solar Hijri calendar
month. This avoids changing allowance merely because a calendar month has a
different length. Display dates may still use the Solar Hijri calendar.

### `V33-DEC-010` — Allowance consumption, return and overage

**Structure ratified 2026-09-02**, with one correction to the recommendation
below: customer-cancellation and no-show treatment is versioned policy rather
than a fixed rule, so no cutoff or retained outcome is hardcoded and the exact
values remain open under #46. The binding structural wording is in the decision
register.

**Recommendation.** Consume one unit atomically when a booking first becomes
`confirmed`, keyed idempotently by booking id. `pending` and `expired` consume
nothing. `completed`, customer-cancelled, late-cancelled and `no_show` keep the
unit consumed because capacity was reserved. Provider/platform cancellation
returns one unit exactly once. Reschedule transfers the same unit and never
consumes a second one.

At 80% and 100%, notify authorised seller roles. Reaching zero credits must not
make existing bookings unreadable or unmanageable. New confirmation after the
limit follows one approved action:

- **Recommended:** seller explicitly buys any permitted additional quantity at
  the then-current snapshotted price; no silent charge;
- alternative: allow a defined grace quantity, then block new confirmation;
- rejected: silently bill an unspecified overage or cancel existing bookings.

**Values to approve:** minimum top-up quantity `____`; optional grace quantity
`____`; subscription payment grace period `____` days.

### `V33-DEC-011` — Allowed collection modes by seller and service

**Recommendation.** Platform policy determines which modes a plan/vertical may
use; an entitled seller chooses a default, and may narrow it per service.
Customers never override the seller's accepted policy.

- `pay_at_venue`: BeauClick collects zero service money;
- `deposit_online_balance_at_venue`: BeauClick collects only the deposit and
  displays the balance payable to the seller;
- `full_payment_online`: BeauClick collects the full service price.

The rollout flag, entitlement, seller policy and operational kill switch must
all pass independently. Disabling online collection falls back only to a mode
the seller explicitly enabled; it never silently changes an accepted booking.

### `V33-DEC-012` — Deposit bounds and rounding

**Recommendation.** Permit either a fixed amount or a percentage, bounded by
the platform. Percentage calculation remains integer basis points, rounds down
to toman, applies min/max, and can never exceed the full service total.

### `V33-DEC-013` — Cancellation, reschedule and no-show outcome

**Recommendation.** Launch with one clear customer cutoff and one reschedule
transfer. The immutable booking snapshot controls the result.

| Fact | Recommended outcome | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| customer cancels at least 24h before start | refund 100% of platform-collected service money | `[ ]` |
| customer cancels less than 24h before start | seller may retain 100% of deposit, never uncollected venue balance | `[ ]` |
| customer no-show, marked only after slot end | seller may retain 100% of deposit | `[ ]` |
| provider cancels | refund 100% of platform-collected service money | `[ ]` |
| platform/payment fault | refund 100% of platform-collected service money | `[ ]` |
| first eligible reschedule before cutoff | transfer deposit; no second charge | `[ ]` |
| later or late reschedule | apply the snapshotted cancellation rule | `[ ]` |

**Values to approve:** cutoff `____` hours; included reschedules `____`; whether

**Legal gate.** Legal must approve the terminology, evidence standard, consumer

### `V33-DEC-014` — Dispute window and moderation authority

**Recommendation.** A customer may dispute a late-cancel/no-show retention for
72 hours after the recorded outcome. Opening a dispute moves only the disputed
platform-held amount from pending to disputed. It cannot change booking history
or charge the uncollected venue balance.

Moderators may uphold customer refund, uphold seller retention, or request
evidence; every read and decision is capability-gated and audited. No moderator
may edit payment, booking or message history.

**Values to approve:** dispute window `____` hours; evidence retention
`____` months; appeal availability and window `____`.

**Legal/privacy gate.** Evidence categories, retention, access and copy require
legal/privacy approval. Medical treatment records are excluded.

### `V33-DEC-015` — Seller availability and settlement delay

**Recommendation.** Platform-captured seller money is `pending` until the later
of the service outcome and the dispute window. An open dispute makes it
`disputed`. A final seller-favouring outcome makes it `available`; a payout rail
makes it `settled`. Refunds and reversals remain append-only counter-entries.

- normal availability: `72` hours after completed service;
- retained late-cancel/no-show deposit: `72` hours after the outcome;
- payout schedule after availability: `________`;
- minimum payout amount: `________` toman;

### `V33-DEC-016` — Platform revenue on each money path

**Recommendation.** Subscription is primary. Separately version these optional
charges; a disabled value is explicitly zero, not an absent default.

| Revenue component | Recommended basis |
|---|---|
| subscription | plan version and term |
| payment processing recovery | platform-collected amount only |
| acquisition fee | disabled at launch; if enabled, first qualifying platform-attributed booking only |
| marketplace commission | disabled for venue-collected balance; optional only on authoritative platform capture |
| SMS/AI/add-on usage | entitlement/credit pack, separately priced |
| retail/B2B | later order policy, not booking commission |

Approve the launch rates: processing `____` bp/fixed; acquisition `____`;
full-online commission `____` bp; deposit commission `____` bp; taxes and
invoice treatment `____`.

**Accounting gate.** Revenue recognition, tax and invoice treatment require
qualified accounting/legal approval. Financial code may represent exact
snapshots before production activation but must not label an unapproved amount
as earned revenue.

### `V33-DEC-017` — Customer-facing policy-copy version

Every confirmation screen and receipt must state, before payment:

- full service price;
- amount BeauClick collects now;
- balance payable at venue;
- cancellation cutoff and retained amount;
- no-show outcome;
- reschedule treatment;
- dispute window;
- seller identity and support route;
- policy-copy version accepted.

**Recommended draft meaning, not approved legal copy:** "You pay only the shown
deposit online. The remaining balance is paid directly to the service provider.
If you cancel before the displayed deadline, the online amount is refunded. A
late cancellation or no-show may cause the displayed deposit to be retained
under the policy you accept before confirmation."

Legal must provide/approve the Persian production copy and version identifier.

## What can proceed before final closure

- contract and deterministic sandbox tests;
- plan/policy version persistence with production activation false;
- authorization, privacy, audit, idempotency and concurrency infrastructure;
- operator readiness reporting that says external/legal gates remain false;
- design exploration explicitly labelled non-production and decision-gated.

The following may not proceed as production-enabled behaviour:

- charging a subscription price;
- collecting or retaining a real deposit;
- marking seller money available or settled;
- applying a commission/acquisition fee;
- displaying unapproved legal copy;
- calling a real gateway, bank or payout provider.
