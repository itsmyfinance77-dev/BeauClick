# V3.1 Product Roadmap

**Status:** PLANNING ONLY. No code written, no feature started, no migration created, no
tag cut. `v3.0.1` and `v3.0.0` untouched.

**Baseline:** `68d3d5e` = `origin/master` = `v3.0.1`.

**Companion documents:** `V3.1_GAP_RECONCILIATION.md` (the evidence),
`V3.1_UIUX_BACKLOG.md`, `V3.1_RELEASE_STRATEGY.md`, and
`V3.1_EXTERNAL_ENABLEMENT_STRATEGY.md` (the owner-approved execution split between
implementation/sandbox readiness and production enablement). The deferred product programme
is now ordered in `../v3.2/V3.2_PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md`.

---

## 1. The one-paragraph judgement

V3.0.1 is a **backend-complete, frontend-partial** platform. Fourteen services, seventeen
controllers, thirteen schemas, 378 real-PostgreSQL tests, an append-only ledger enforced
by database roles, a Persian-analyzed OpenSearch read model, a transactional outbox with
five independent consumers, and an ownership model that resolves every party server-side
from the session. It is genuinely good engineering. It is also, right now, **not
operable**: no account can hold an administrative capability, no professional can publish
availability or complete a booking through the product, no professional can be verified,
no customer can leave a review, no image exists anywhere, and no real money can move.
V3.1's job is not to add domains. It is to **make what exists usable**, and to close the
three structurally inert trust signals that make the marketplace's own ranking formula
mostly decorative.

---

## 2. What V3.1 is, stated as constraints

**V3.1 will:** complete the customer and professional product loops on top of the API that
already exists; make the platform administrable; make verification, ratings, and imagery
real; make the product legally presentable; and close out production-payment enablement.

**V3.1 will not:** introduce AI, internal chat, referrals, wishlist, CRM, B2B quotes,
campaigns, calendar sync, automated payout, Kafka, Redis-backed anything, or ClickHouse.
Each is deferred with a stated reason in `V3.1_GAP_RECONCILIATION.md` §4 and ordered for
later work in `../v3.2/V3.2_PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md`.

**V3.1 must not:** require an architectural rewrite, break an existing API contract,
violate a schema-ownership boundary, reintroduce WordPress or WooCommerce, create a second
engine for anything that already has one, or add a cross-service table read. §17 tests
every proposed feature against those five rules.

## 3. Core product loop — where the chain breaks

```
Discovery ──> Search ──> Provider ──> Service ──> Auth ──> Booking ──> Payment ──> Receipt
    ✅          ✅          ⚠️           ⚠️        ✅         ⚠️          ⛔          ✅
                                                                                     │
   Retention <── Referral <── Review <── Journey <── Loyalty <────────────────────────┘
      ⚠️            ⛔          ⛔          ✅          ✅
```

| Link | State | What is actually missing |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | ✅ | Homepage is an honest placeholder, not a designed entry point (QA-16r) |
| Search | ✅ | Works well. Ranking is flatter than designed because two of its inputs are inert |
| Provider | ⚠️ | Profiles render, but **no images, no portfolio, no verification badge that can ever be earned** |
| Service | ⚠️ | Services exist and are bookable — but only a professional can create them, and no professional has a UI to do it |
| Auth | ✅ | Sound. **But no SMS is actually delivered** (GAP-11), so nobody can log in off-platform |
| Booking | ⚠️ | Customer side works. **Completion, no-show, and reschedule are professional actions with no UI**, so a booking can never reach `completed` |
| Payment | ⛔ | Sandbox only. `GAP-06b` |
| Receipt | ✅ | Correct, semantic, accessible |
| Loyalty | ✅ | Real. Points award on booking completion — which cannot happen (see Booking) |
| Journey | ✅ | Real and privacy-boundaried |
| Review | ⛔ | Does not exist |
| Referral | ⛔ | Does not exist |
| Retention | ⚠️ | Notification centre is real; off-platform delivery is not |

**The single most damaging break is not the obvious one.** It is that `BookingCompleted`
is unreachable through the product, because completion is a professional action and
professionals have no interface. That one missing surface silently disables loyalty point
awards, journey timeline composition, the `completed_bookings` ranking signal, review
eligibility (once reviews exist), and referral qualification (once referrals exist). Five
downstream domains are waiting on one button.

## 5. Trust and retention — ranked by real user impact

1. **Reviews** — the primary conversion signal in this category, and the only one that
   makes the existing ranking formula do its job.
2. **Verification** (R31-02) — the second trust signal, already 80% built, currently
   unreachable.
3. **Imagery** — a beauty marketplace where customers cannot see work. The UI/UX audit
   calls this the most consequential gap after the font, and it is right.
4. **Real SMS delivery** (GAP-11) — not usually a "trust" item, but here it is the login
   door. Nobody can authenticate without it.
5. **Privacy export/deletion** — trust in the legal sense, and the one item with genuine
   regulatory weight.
6. **The font** (QA-17) — every Persian character on every screen.
7. **Footer / terms / privacy / support** — the trust furniture a payment-handling
   marketplace is expected to have.
8. **Resend OTP** (QA-19) — the auth screen's only dead end.
9. **Error/empty/loading states** — already systematically fixed in `v3.0.1`; the
   remaining work is the component layer that stops them recurring.
10. **Referral, wishlist** — real retention levers with nothing to amplify yet.

## 6. Search and discovery — what V3.1 should and should not do

The V3 search is genuinely strong: real Persian analyzer chain (Arabic↔Persian folding,
ZWNJ removal, Persian and Arabic-Indic digit folding), fuzzy matching with transpositions,
edge-ngram autocomplete with a non-expanding search analyzer, revision-ordered projection,
facets, and a Bayesian-shrunk ranking formula with cold-start blending. **Fuzzy search,
Persian normalization, and autocomplete are already done and verified against a live
OpenSearch 2.19.1** — they are not V3.1 work.

| Candidate | Verdict for V3.1 |
|---|---|
| Fuzzy search | **Already done.** GAP-14 closed. |
| Persian normalization | **Already done.** |
| Autocomplete | **Already done.** |
| Ranking improvements | **Yes — but by fixing inputs, not the formula.** Two of five ranking terms (rating, verified) are permanently zero. Fixing the producers (D and A) improves ranking more than any weight change could. |
| Synonyms | **No.** No evidence of need. Revisit with real query logs. |
| Geo-aware ranking | **No.** City filtering exists; distance ranking needs coordinates that no entity carries. New scope. |
| Availability-aware search | **Evidence-gated.** `AvailabilityPublished` already has an optional-consumer note in `V3_EVENT_CATALOG.md`. Defer until B produces real availability data. |
| Search analytics | **Partial yes.** `SearchPerformed` is already emitted with query text redacted. Surfacing zero-result rate and click-through in the admin analytics screen is S-sized and directly informs whether synonyms are ever needed. **Phase A.** |
| Search quality metrics | Same — fold into the admin analytics screen. |

**Recommendation: no new search engineering in V3.1 beyond wiring the two dead signals and
surfacing zero-result rate.** The engine is ahead of the data it indexes.

## 7. AI — classification

**Verdict: V3.2+. Not V3.1.**

- **Current state:** verified absent by exhaustive search. No service, module, controller,
  entity, or schema. `bc_use_ai_assistant` is a capability with nothing behind it. Every
  filename match was an `.nx/cache` artifact.
- **Groundwork that exists:** `V3_DOMAIN_BOUNDARIES.md` §ai is a complete specification —
  schema, two-stage authorization, curated-context-only data access, the explicit rule
  that free-text/PII fields (`beauty_profiles.notes`, review text, CRM notes) are excluded
  by construction rather than access-controlled within. `journey.inferAiDefaults()` exists
  as a real, tested seam (ADR-019). `GAP-12` (one thread per tenant) is explicitly flagged
  to be revisited rather than re-adopted.
- **Why not V3.1:** it is the largest single new domain in the set (provider abstraction,
  conversation model, curation layer, output validation, safety gates, usage limits, cost
  controls), it is **blocked on the hosting/region decision** (Anthropic-class API
  reachability from Iranian infrastructure is restricted —
  `V3_INFRASTRUCTURE_PLAN.md` §10), and it adds no value on top of a product whose supply
  side cannot operate. Building the assistant before the professional dashboard would be
  building the demo before the product.
- **What V3.1 should do about AI:** nothing except **not foreclose it**. The seam is fixed,
  the boundary is specified, and no V3.1 work touches either.

## 8. Admin platform — verdict and minimum scope

**Verdict: yes, V3.1, and first.** Not because admin is glamorous but because R31-01 means
the platform currently cannot be operated at all, and three other V3.1 items (verification,
review moderation, settlement) require a privileged principal that no account can become.

**Minimum operational capability set for V3.1:**

| Capability | Backend state | Why minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Role / capability grant | **Missing** (R31-01) | Nothing else in this table is reachable without it |
| Professional verification queue | State machine + event exist; no route | Unblocks the `verified` signal, badge, and filter |
| Audit log viewer | **Missing** (GAP-02-V3) | The first privileged principal must be attributable from day one |
| Financial settlement + reversal | API complete, gated | Professionals cannot be paid without it |
| Search index health + reindex | API complete, gated | The only recovery path for a corrupted index |
| Notification delivery status + retry | API complete, gated | The only way to see that SMS is silently not delivering |
| Phone-conflict resolution (GAP-20) | Table exists, `resolved_at` write-never | Small, and the data is already being captured |
| Loyalty policy visibility (GAP-10) | API complete, gated | The mechanism that makes provisional values visible |

**Deliberately out of the minimum:** ranking-weight tuning (GAP-24 — defer until there is
ranking data worth tuning), review moderation (Phase D), user impersonation (never),
business management beyond read (ADR-023 scope), bulk operations.

**Architecture note:** `V3_FRONTEND_ARCHITECTURE.md` §10 leaves `apps/admin`
separate-app-vs-route-group open. **Recommendation: a route group inside `apps/web`**, not
a second Next.js app. Rationale: one shared design system, one auth/refresh implementation,
one deploy, and the capability gate is already enforced server-side on every route so the
bundle-separation argument buys little. Revisit if admin grows past ~15 screens.

## 9. Privacy and legal

Three things are being conflated in the current documents and should be separated:

| | What it is | Who decides | V3.1? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical requirement** | Per-module `exportSubjectData(userId)` / `eraseSubjectData(userId)`, self-registering so a new module cannot be forgotten; a `privacy.data_requests` lifecycle table; anonymization for records that must survive erasure (ledger entries are append-only and legally must persist) | Engineering | **Yes, Phase E** |
| **Legal / business decision** | Terms of service, privacy policy, retention periods, deletion grace period (GAP-21), what "deleted" means for financial records, whether professionals/businesses get export too (GAP-22 says customer-only in V2) | Business + legal counsel | **Yes — and it is on the critical path, so start it in week one** |
| **Optional enhancement** | Self-service download of a prior export, per-category privacy toggles beyond notification preferences, consent audit trail | Product | **No — V3.2+** |

**The hard technical question, stated now so it is not discovered later:** the financial
ledger is append-only and enforced by database role grants. `eraseSubjectData` cannot
delete a ledger row and must not try. Erasure therefore has to be **anonymization at the
identity layer with referential integrity preserved downstream** — the `user_id` a ledger
entry references keeps existing as a tombstone row. That design must be settled before any
module implements its erase contract, or fourteen modules will each invent a different
answer.

## 10. Payment and production readiness

**Should V3.1 include real gateway integration? Yes — and it should be the last thing
merged, not the first.**

Reasoning: it is the only remaining production blocker that is pure engineering plus
credentials, everything around it is already built and proven, and `EXC-001`'s own review
condition fires at `v3.1.0` regardless. Deferring it again means cutting a third release
under an exception that does not cover it.

**Required architecture — nothing new is needed:**

- `PaymentProvider` interface exists and the sandbox implements the same contract a real
  adapter will.
- The registry refuses unknown provider keys rather than falling back — a misconfigured
  `PAYMENT_DEFAULT_PROVIDER` cannot silently reroute payments.
- `VerifyPaymentResult.paidCurrency` is already rule 3 of the provider contract, required
  and matched, **failing closed when absent** (QA-10). This is the single most important
  piece of adapter-readiness work and it is already done.
- Server-to-server `verify()` already ignores callback parameters entirely.
- Duplicate-charge detection and auto-refund already exist; CAS on decide and refund
  already exist.

**External prerequisites, none of which are engineering:**

1. Gateway selection (Zarinpal / IDPay / NextPay / Mellat-class) — a business decision.
2. Real merchant credentials, held as deployment secrets.
3. **The hosting decision**, because the gateway must be able to reach the callback URL.
4. A sandbox-test cycle against the *gateway's own* sandbox — real money units, real field
   semantics, real error codes, real network failure modes. The local simulator does not
   substitute and never claimed to.
5. Real target PostgreSQL host verified with `database/scripts/financial-roles.sql`
   (`HOSTING_GRANTS`) and `PHASE4-03` handled.
6. A live browser transaction against the gateway sandbox, then one controlled real-money
   transaction, before general availability.
7. `GAP-06b` formally closed on that evidence and `EXC-001` **retired, not lapsed**.

**Bundle QA-21 into this work.** The `failureCode` redirect contract should be designed
once, with the real adapter, rather than changed twice.

**Money-unit discipline is the top adapter risk.** Iranian gateways commonly denominate in
rials; 1 toman = 10 rials. QA-10 already made the check unit-aware. The adapter must map
explicitly and be tested against both units, and the mapping must live in the adapter, not
leak into `applyVerification`.

## 14. Prioritization

Scored on user value, business value, trust, revenue, retention, security, operational
value, technical leverage, against complexity, dependencies, and risk. **Not** scored on
implementation ease — B and A are both large, and both rank P0.

|---|---|---|

## 15. Recommended V3.1 phases

Seven phases. The structure deviates from the brief's example in three deliberate ways,
each justified by evidence: **A leads with operability rather than customer completion**,
because nothing else is administrable without it; **B is professional-first rather than
customer-first**, because the customer loop is already the more complete of the two; and
**G runs concurrently rather than last**, because building B on today's component layer
manufactures the sixth instance of two known bug classes.

---

### V3.1-A — Operability Foundation

**Goal.** The platform can be administered by a real, least-privileged, fully audited
principal, and a professional can be verified.

**Features.** Dynamic `identity.roles` / `identity.capabilities` with a grant path and a
documented bootstrap procedure · verification request/evidence/history tables and
submit → queue → decide routes · DB-persisted `admin.admin_audit_log` with structural
registration enforcement in `libs/audit` · admin route group in `apps/web` covering the
eight minimum screens in §8 · phone-conflict resolution (GAP-20) · search zero-result and
click-through metrics on the analytics screen.

**Dependencies.** None. Evidence upload defers to C (submit-without-evidence works first).

**APIs.** New: `POST /v1/verification/submit`, `GET /v1/admin/verification/queue`,
`POST /v1/admin/verification/:id/decide`, `POST /v1/admin/users/:id/roles`,
`GET /v1/admin/audit-log`, `POST /v1/admin/phone-conflicts/:id/resolve`. Existing admin
routes gain a UI, not a change.

**Database.** `identity.roles`, `identity.capabilities`, `identity.user_roles`;
`provider.verification_requests`, `_evidence`, `_history`; `admin.admin_audit_log`
(append-only, same role-grant mechanism as `financial.ledger_entries` — ADR-009's pattern,
reused not reinvented).

**Events.** `ProfessionalVerificationChanged` already exists and is already consumed by
search. `RoleGranted` / `RoleRevoked` v1 are new and feed analytics + audit.

**Security.** This phase creates the platform's first privileged principal. Non-negotiables:
default new privileged accounts to `platform_operator`, never `administrator`
(`V3_SECURITY_MODEL.md` §9); audit-log every grant with actor, before-state, after-state,
and reason; the audit table's append-only guarantee enforced by database role, not
convention; no impersonation feature, ever; the bootstrap procedure documented and
single-use rather than a seeded superuser row.

**Tests.** Adversarial: a `customer` cannot reach any `/v1/admin/*` route; a
`platform_operator` cannot grant `administrator`; a granted role is revocable and the
revocation takes effect on the **next** token, with the window documented; an admin route
registered without an audit call **fails to boot** (structural, per the V2 fix that closed
GAP-02); the verification state machine rejects every illegal transition over HTTP, not
just in-service.

**Live QA.** Grant a role, verify a professional end to end in a browser, confirm the
`verified` badge appears in search results and the `verifiedOnly` filter narrows.

**Definition of Done.** A named human can be granted `platform_operator` through a
documented procedure; every admin action they take appears in a queryable audit log; a
professional can be verified and the badge, filter, and ranking term all change as a
result; `WEIGHT_VERIFIED` is demonstrably non-zero for at least one provider.

### V3.1-B — Professional Operating Surface

**Goal.** A professional can run their business through the product, and
`BookingCompleted` becomes reachable.

**Features.** `/pro` route group: profile create/edit, service catalogue CRUD, availability
management (individual slots + bulk generation + deletion), booking list with
complete / no-show / reschedule / history, earnings (`/v1/me/finance/*` — summary,
outstanding orders, settlements, per-order ledger), analytics · staff invite by phone
(#18) · QA-25 nav conditioning, which falls out of A.

**Dependencies.** None on the backend. Benefits materially from G running concurrently.

**APIs.** **Zero new endpoints.** Every route this phase consumes is already built, gated
by an ownership resolver, and covered by real-PostgreSQL tests. The one addition is
`POST /v1/businesses/:id/staff/invite-by-phone` for #18.

**Database.** None, except the invite-by-phone path if #18 is approved.

**Events.** None new. `BookingCompleted` starts firing for real, which activates five
already-wired consumers.

**Security.** Every route already resolves the party server-side from the session. The
risk in this phase is **frontend**, not backend: never let a professional id, business id,
or party id reach a request body from client state. The ownership resolvers will reject it,
but a UI that constructs such a request is a UI that will be refactored into a bug.

**Tests.** Component tests for the availability editor's timezone and DST handling (the
one genuinely subtle piece); e2e coverage of the full professional journey; a regression
test asserting that loyalty points, journey timeline, and the `completed_bookings` ranking
signal all move when a booking is completed **through the UI**.

**Live QA.** The full loop in a real browser: register → become a professional → publish
availability → receive a customer booking → complete it → see earnings change. This is the
first time that loop has ever been runnable.

**Definition of Done.** A professional performs every capability in §4's table that has an
API, without touching an API client. A booking reaches `completed` through the product and
the five downstream consumers all observably react.

### V3.1-C — Media, Portfolio, Imagery

**Goal.** The marketplace becomes visual.

**Features.** Object-storage adapter (S3-compatible) with presigned direct upload ·
image validation, resizing, and format normalization · `provider.portfolio_items`
redesigned from requirements per GAP-23, **not ported** · professional avatar and cover
image · verification evidence upload wired back into A · `next/image` with explicit
aspect ratios throughout search results, profile pages, and booking cards.

**Dependencies.** Hosting decision (#1) → storage provider. A (for evidence).

**APIs.** `POST /v1/media/upload-url` (presigned), `POST /v1/providers/:id/portfolio`,
`DELETE /v1/providers/:id/portfolio/:itemId`, `PATCH /v1/providers/:id/avatar`.

**Database.** `provider.portfolio_items`, plus media-reference columns on `professionals`.

**Events.** `PortfolioItemAdded` v1 → search reindex (a provider with work shown is a
different search result than one without).

**Security.** This is where `V3_SECURITY_MODEL.md` §8's protected-file model becomes real.
Verification evidence is sensitive personal documentation and must **never** be publicly
addressable — including after a backup restore. Portfolio images are public by design.
Two different access classes, one storage layer: get that boundary right before either
ships. Also required: content-type validation server-side (never trusting the client),
size caps, per-professional quotas, and an abuse-report path.

**Tests.** A presigned URL cannot be used to write outside its own key prefix; evidence is
unreachable without the moderation capability; an oversized or wrong-type upload is
rejected server-side; a deleted portfolio item's object is actually deleted.

**Live QA.** Upload from a real phone browser at 375px. Confirm zero layout shift with

### V3.1-D — Reviews and the Rating Signal

**Goal.** The rating half of the ranking formula stops being decorative.

**Features.** `provider.reviews` with eligibility gated on a real `BookingCompleted` for
that customer/professional pair · one review per completed booking · professional response ·
moderation queue on A's capability model · `ReviewCreated` v1 contract · the
`search.ranking_signals` writer that closes QA-18 · frontend: submission, display on
profile and search results, rating filter and sort finally exposed · a shared enum or lint
rule for label maps (QA-22).

**Dependencies.** A (moderation capability), B (completion must be reachable), C (reviews
next to portfolio work is the point).

**APIs.** `POST /v1/bookings/:id/review`, `GET /v1/providers/:id/reviews`,
`POST /v1/providers/:id/reviews/:reviewId/respond`, `GET /v1/admin/reviews/queue`,
`POST /v1/admin/reviews/:id/moderate`.

**Database.** `provider.reviews`. No change to `search.ranking_signals` — the columns and

**Live QA.** Complete a booking, leave a review, watch the provider's badge and search
position change.

**Definition of Done.** `search.ranking_signals.review_count > 0` for a real provider;

### V3.1-E — Trust, Legal, and Retention Surfaces

**Features.** Vazirmatn self-hosted subset (QA-17) · footer with terms, privacy, contact,

## 16. Recommended implementation order

```
Week 1        DECISIONS START:  #1 hosting · #14 legal content · #15 font · #2 gateway
              ENGINEERING START: B (professional surface) + G (component layer), concurrent
                                 — neither needs a decision or infrastructure

then          A (operability foundation)
                — can start immediately too if capacity allows; it has no upstream
                  dependency either, and B/A are the only two phases that don't

then          C (media/portfolio)          ← gated on #1 producing a storage provider
              E-partial: font, footer      ← gated on #15 and #14

then          D (reviews)                  ← needs A (moderation) and B (completion)

then          E-remainder: privacy, SMS port, homepage, SEO, auth polish
              → continue every implementable/backend/design/UI item
              → isolate legal copy, font, live SMS, and credentials as explicit gates

then          F-implementable: provider-neutral hardening, failureCode, deployment/secret
              contracts, observability, backup/restore automation, sandbox tests

then          Final UI implementation + global UI/UX, accessibility, browser, security,
              and regression QA

milestone     IMPLEMENTATION COMPLETE / SANDBOX READY

last          EXTERNAL ENABLEMENT GATE     ← hosting, gateway/vendor credentials, live SMS,
                                             real-host grants, restore drill, production
                                             telemetry, controlled real-money transaction

              → PRODUCTION ENABLED / RELEASE READY → release
```

**Why B and G first rather than A.** A is architecturally prior — verification, moderation,
and settlement all need it. But B has zero dependencies, needs zero new API, unblocks five
downstream domains through a single missing button, and is the difference between a demo
and a marketplace. Running B and A in parallel is possible and preferable; if only one can
proceed, **B produces more product per unit of risk**, because every endpoint it consumes
is already built, gated, and tested against real PostgreSQL.

**Why F production enablement remains last.** Every live prerequisite is external, the
exception question (R31-04) must be settled regardless, and enabling a real-money path in a
product whose supply side cannot yet operate would be enabling payments for a marketplace
with nothing to buy. The provider-neutral and sandbox-testable part of F may now be built
earlier; only the live enablement gate remains last.

**Why external enablement no longer blocks implementation order.** The 2026-08-29 owner
decision separates code/design/sandbox readiness from production evidence. Work continues
around external blockers, but the blockers remain open and the strict Phase F Definition of
Done still controls production. The full classification and evidence rules are in
`V3.1_EXTERNAL_ENABLEMENT_STRATEGY.md`.

## 17. Architecture compatibility check

Every proposed V3.1 feature, tested against the five prohibitions in §2.

| Feature | Rewrite? | Breaking API change? | Ownership violation? | WP/Woo? | Duplicate engine? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Verification workflow | No — the state machine, its CAS, and its event already exist | No — additive routes | No — `provider` owns it | No | No | ✅ |
| Admin route group | No | No | No — composes each module's own gated routes; reads no other module's tables | No | No | ✅ |

| Professional frontend | No | No — **consumes existing endpoints unchanged** | No | No | No | ✅ |
| Object storage + portfolio | No | Additive | `provider` owns portfolio | No | No | ✅ |

- **Business as a bookable party with its own calendar.** Would require touching
  `booking`'s `professionalId` identity concept and the three-layer slot-claim concurrency

  the reasoning has not changed.
- **A second analytics/metrics engine for admin dashboards.** `MetricsService.compute()` is

- **Reading `provider.professionals` from `search` for reindex.** Search consumes events and
  its own projection. A direct cross-schema read would be a boundary violation the Nx lint
  rule would catch anyway.
- **Any Redis/Kafka adoption "while we're in there."** ADR-022 deferred both on evidence.
  Redis returns only if the hosting topology produces multiple API instances.

## 20. First implementation task — exact recommendation

**Do not start with code.**

**Task 0 (same day, non-engineering):** open the hosting/region decision (#1) with a named
owner and a date, and commission the legal content (#14). Both are on the critical path,
both have external lead time, and both have already slipped past two releases. Nothing
else in this plan is schedulable until #1 has an answer.

**Task 1 (the first implementation task):**

> **Build the professional availability and booking-management surface: the `/pro` route
> group covering availability publishing (`GET/POST/DELETE /v1/me/availability/slots`,
> `POST /v1/me/availability/slots/bulk`), the professional booking list
> (`GET /v1/me/professional-bookings`), and the three lifecycle actions
> (`POST /v1/bookings/:id/complete`, `/no-show`, `/reschedule`) — introducing the
> `TextLink` and `PageHeader` primitives from V3.1-G as it goes, rather than another
> screenful of inline `style={{}}`.**

Why this exact task, and not a larger or a more foundational one:

1. **Zero dependencies.** No decision, no infrastructure, no credential, no migration, no
   new endpoint. It can start today.
2. **It is the highest-leverage single change in the plan.** `BookingCompleted` is
   currently unreachable through the product, and five already-wired consumers — loyalty
   points, journey timeline, the `completed_bookings` ranking signal, review eligibility,
   and referral qualification — are all waiting on it.
3. **The expensive, risky half is already built.** Every endpoint is gated by an ownership
   resolver, covered by real-PostgreSQL tests, and adversarially tested for cross-tenant
   forgery. This is UI work over a proven contract, which is the lowest-risk large task
   available.
4. **It proves the plan's central claim** — that V3.1's biggest win is surfacing built
   capability rather than building new capability. If that claim is wrong, this task is
   where it fails cheaply.
5. **It seeds V3.1-G naturally.** Two primitives extracted in the course of real work,
   rather than a speculative component-library phase.

**Definition of Done for Task 1.** A professional publishes availability, receives a real
customer booking, and marks it complete — entirely through the browser — and loyalty points,
the journey timeline entry, and the `completed_bookings` ranking signal all observably move
as a result. That single trace has never been runnable in V3's history, and running it is
the most informative thing V3.1 can do first.
