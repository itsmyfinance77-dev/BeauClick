## 4. Decision-status matrix

### Binding — 15 architecture decisions (قطعی)
**No conflict was found** between the DOCX's 15 binding decisions and the corresponding register cards, and no DOCX item presented an open value as settled. A clause-by-clause equivalence check of all 25 remaining questions against the register was not performed, so no stronger claim than "no conflict found" is made. Revenue model (no separate customer booking fee); server-determined seller party;
seller frozen at booking, later affiliation changes nothing; exactly three collection modes;
deposit is a calculation rule, not a fourth mode; policy change only by publishing a new immutable
version; no retroactivity; retained amount never exceeds money actually collected; before a policy
is approved no penalty/commission/expiry activates and the safe state is refund; one credit consumed
at first confirmation, booking-id idempotent; return is an independent immutable idempotent record;
price and allowance are administrator-set, versioned, never a code constant; monetary credit granted
only after proven payment in the same transaction; no payment/receivable/refund/settlement record for
money never collected; activation, entitlement, business policy and emergency stop are four
independent controls.

## 5. Backend-support matrix

**Implemented:** plan/schedule/collection-policy publication lifecycle with audit and activation
windows (27 admin routes) · seller workspace list, plan selection, cancellation, history ·
credit-purchase quote, record and history · credit accounting with selective enforcement ·
collection-policy assignment · three-amount receipt · sandbox deposit capture · zero-collectible
confirmation · business classification, locations, resources, service resource requirements ·
workspace-scoped seller finance reads.

**Implemented since the previous pass:** order-level policy resolution and immutable per-order snapshot (#115) · rollout state, per-party governance fact, aggregate preview, emergency kill switch and global activation (#95, #141).

**Backend implemented, frontend not built:** pre-confirmation three-amount disclosure · collection-mode-aware checkout · the enforcement control-plane operator screens · a seller-facing read of the party's own governance state (no route exposes it). Partial refund remains structural only, because no policy publishes a retained amount.

**Deliberately absent from the order read:** `policyKey`, `policyVersion` and `policyAcceptedAt` are not returned by `GET /v1/orders/:id`. The customer sees the three amounts, never the policy identifiers.

**Blocked — external:** real collection, payout, provider activation (#47) · credit grant on
verified payment (#99, and no gateway adapter exists in the repository at all) · commission,
pending funds, settlement release, reversal (#43).

**Blocked — legal:** cancellation cutoff, late-cancellation and no-show retention, reschedule
action, dispute window, settlement delay, customer policy copy and its version (#42).

**Not planned as code yet:** the entire dispute domain · scoped staff authority beyond chat ·
scoped read-only business finance for a staff grant (#44e/#111) · retail and B2B seller surfaces ·
finance/support and dispute-resolution operator tiers.

## 8. Accessibility verification

Checked against `26_ACCESSIBILITY_SPEC.md` and applied to every new section:

- **Status never relies on colour alone.** Every badge pairs its colour with a distinct shape
  (filled circle, square, dashed ring, outlined square) plus its Persian text label.
- **Targets:** every button, input, textarea and radio/checkbox row in the new sections is ≥44px
  in its interactive dimension.
- **Contrast:** body ink `oklch(0.22 0.02 330)` and muted `oklch(0.46 0.02 330)` on white or
  `oklch(0.985 …)` surfaces; the error family uses full-opacity `oklch(0.5 0.16 25)` on
  `oklch(0.98 0.02 25)`; no alpha-muted or `color-mix` body text anywhere. Faint
  `oklch(0.58 0.015 330)` is used for labels and captions at ≥12px, never for the only copy of a
  consequence.
- **Semantics:** the disclosure block is a `<dl>`; ledger and catalogue tables are real tables with
  `<caption>`/`<thead>`; the deposit rule and collection mode are `<fieldset>`/`<legend>` radio
  groups; dialogs carry `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal` and `aria-labelledby`; the disabled
  acceptance control carries `aria-describedby` pointing at the reason it is disabled.
- **Disabled controls always state why** in adjacent text, never by tooltip alone.
- **RTL:** `dir="rtl"` at the container; `dir="ltr"` with `unicode-bidi: isolate` on keys,
  references and code; logical properties (`padding-inline-start`, `text-align: start`) throughout.
- **Not claimed:** no WCAG 2.1 AA conformance statement is made — consistent with this project's
  standing caution. Contrast was reasoned from the token values, not measured with a tool, and the
  prototypes are static design artifacts with no keyboard-trap or screen-reader run-through.
- **Non-enumerating errors:** every refusal in the new sections is the single shared sentence with
  no cause, count or distinction — matching the server.

## 11. Requires testing at frontend implementation — not claimed as PASS

These cannot be verified in a static design artifact and are recorded as implementation acceptance criteria rather than results:

- Tab order through every form, dialog and table in a real DOM.
- Focus trap inside each dialog while open, and focus return to the invoking control on close.
- Screen-reader announcement order in RTL, including mixed LTR key and code runs.
- Live-region announcement of state transitions (saving, saved, refused).
- Measured contrast ratios with a tool, per token pair, at final rendered sizes.
- Keyboard operation of the quantity input, the deposit-rule fieldset and the collection-mode radio group.
- Touch-target size at real device pixel ratios, not CSS pixels alone.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` behaviour once any transition exists.
